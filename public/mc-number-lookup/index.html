What an MC number is

An MC (motor carrier) number is the docket for-hire carriers hold for operating authority. This lookup finds the DOT record behind the docket and shows whether that authority is active or inactive.

Before you approve a carrier

Check that authority is active, the registration was updated recently, and the phone, address and email are not shared with unrelated or inactive carriers. Verify insurance and identity independently as well.

Where the data comes from

The FMCSA Company Census File, refreshed daily from a database about 24 hours old.
