California carriers also get CHP inspection results and DMV permit checks when available.

What a DOT number tells you

A USDOT number identifies a carrier in FMCSA's registry. This lookup shows whether the record is active, which operating-authority dockets (MC, MX, FF) are attached, fleet size, the last MCS-150 update, and any safety rating.

What gets cross-checked

Phone numbers, addresses, emails and names are compared against other DOT records. Sharing is common among related companies, so these are leads to review, not findings of fraud.

Where the data comes from

The FMCSA Company Census File, refreshed daily from a database about 24 hours old. Registration data is self-reported and can lag reality.
